Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how NoMoreNight, LLC ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when using With Diary (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

If you have questions, contact us at admin@nomorenight.com.

Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the App's features:

  • ▸Email address — Used to create and authenticate your account. Collected at sign-up via email/password or a third-party auth provider (e.g. Sign in with Apple, Google).
  • ▸Diary entries — The text you write in your diary. Stored privately and associated only with your account.
  • ▸Photos — Images you choose to attach to diary entries. Stored in a private, access-controlled bucket.
  • ▸Account metadata — Timestamps, entry counts, and other lightweight metadata needed to operate the App (no advertising identifiers or device fingerprints).

We do not collect location data, contacts, or any information unrelated to the diary experience.

How Data Is Stored

All data is stored in infrastructure operated by the following sub-processors:

  • ▸Supabase — Provides the database (PostgreSQL) and authentication layer. Your email address, diary text, and account metadata are stored in a Supabase project hosted on AWS infrastructure. Supabase enforces row-level security so that only your account can read your data.
  • ▸Amazon Web Services (AWS) — Photo attachments are stored in a private Amazon S3 bucket. Objects are not publicly accessible and require a signed, time-limited URL generated by Supabase on your behalf.

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored encrypted at rest.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing, analytics, or advertising purposes.

The only third parties that process your data are the sub-processors listed above (Supabase and AWS), which act solely as infrastructure providers under our direction, and the authentication provider you choose at sign-up (Apple or Google), which confirms your identity and supplies your email address.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), all associated personal data — including your email address, diary entries, and photos — is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

Deleting Your Data

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time, at no cost.

How to delete your account:

  1. 01Open With Diary on your device.
  2. 02Navigate to Settings (gear icon in the bottom navigation).
  3. 03Tap "Delete Account".
  4. 04Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Alternatively, you may email admin@nomorenight.com to request deletion and we will process it within 7 business days.

Children's Privacy

With Diary is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. For material changes we will provide additional notice (e.g. an in-app notification).

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at admin@nomorenight.com.
